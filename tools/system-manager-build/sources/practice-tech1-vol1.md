## Study notes for this paper

- Unit I carries 60 of 150 marks — nearly 40%. Generations, classification, memory hierarchy, storage types and the emerging-technology definitions (AI, IoT, blockchain, cloud, edge, big data) are the highest-yield areas.
- The syllabus repeatedly says "(Concept)" or "(Introduction)" for items like virtualization, APIs, Power Query, Power Pivot, Macros and Copilot. Expect definitional questions on these rather than deep technical ones.
- MS Office units together carry 65 marks. Practice on the actual software matters more than reading — especially Excel lookups, pivot tables, what-if tools, and Word's styles/TOC/mail merge chain.
- The AI-assisted topics (Copilot in Word/PowerPoint, AI-assisted spreadsheet analysis) are new additions and are likely to be tested at a conceptual level.
