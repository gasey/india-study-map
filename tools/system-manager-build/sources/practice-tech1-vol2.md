## How to use Volumes 1 and 2

1. **First pass** — attempt each unit closed-book, mark the ones you guessed even when correct. Guessed-correct answers are the real gaps.
2. **Weakest first** — Unit I is 60 marks; if you score below 70% there, that single unit costs more than all of PowerPoint.
3. **Hands-on** — Units III, IV and V total 65 marks and cannot be learned by reading. Sit with Word, Excel and PowerPoint open and reproduce every feature named in the syllabus at least once.
4. **The "(Concept)" markers** — Power Query, Power Pivot, Macros, Copilot, virtualization, APIs. Learn a clean two-line definition and one use case for each. That is the depth the paper is signalling.
5. **Note the syllabus caveat** — the paper may include questions outside the listed topics, drawn from the qualifying degree. Do not treat the syllabus as a hard ceiling for Unit I.
