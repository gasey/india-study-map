## 2. Why the text is missing (verified)

The published paper is an image-only PDF of **7 pages** (producer `iLovePDF`, ModDate `29 Nov 2024`). Page-by-page OCR of the rendered scan shows:

| PDF page | Printed content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Q1–Q10 |
| 2 | Q11–Q24 |
| 3 | Q25–Q38 |
| 4 | Q39–Q52 |
| 5 | Q53–**Q66** (ends at "…causative agent of African Swine Fever") |
| 6 | **Q80**–Q93 (starts at "Which city is called 'the Pink City'?") |
| 7 | Q94–Q100 |

Printed page 6 (Q67–Q79, 13 questions) was never scanned into the file. This is a defect of the source document itself, not of OCR or of the local archive:

- Official PDF SHA-256: `8330d40b314068eeb5372e28f792affdbd1665bc9a7bf30f25ae344e5a23f703` (7 pages).
- The local archive copy `Steno Grade-III General Knowledge- November, 2024..pdf` has the **same** SHA-256.
- A fresh download of the official URL on 9 Oct 2026 is byte-identical (same SHA-256, still 7 pages) — it has **not** been re-uploaded/corrected.
- The Wayback Machine holds **exactly one** capture of that file (timestamp `20250121004232`, digest `YSHDZUPTVGMONABWYMVLOUGP2X2OMPFV`), i.e. the same defective file.
- The project's own Group B review already records: *"Thirteen questions (Stenographer GK November 2024 Q67–79) remain absent from the source."*

The question paper carries a single booklet code (`STENO-III/A/2013-MPSC`); there are no parallel A/B/C/D booklets whose page 6 might survive.

## 5. Conclusion

The question text and options for **Q67–Q79 cannot be recovered from any accessible source.** Every available copy of the paper — official site (current and archival), the local archive, and Scribd — descends from the same 7-page file that omits printed page 6. The missing block sits inside the 2024 current-affairs segment (Q59–Q66 are current affairs and current-affairs/Mizoram material resumes at Q80), so its core is almost certainly unique to this paper and exists in no other questionnaire. A few static-bank items that also recur in the March-2025 paper are conceivable candidates, but nothing in the answer key allows them to be identified or ordered; they are therefore not reported as recovered.

The only authoritative data recovered is the official answer sequence:

> **Q67 A · Q68 B · Q69 C · Q70 B · Q71 B · Q72 B · Q73 A · Q74 B · Q75 B · Q76 C · Q77 A · Q78 D · Q79 D**

To obtain the actual stems/options, MPSC would have to release a corrected scan (or the original page image) of printed page 6. Nothing found so far makes that possible.
